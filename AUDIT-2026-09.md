# Statistical audit, September 2026

A read-only audit of CFU Plot Studio was carried out in September 2026 against
R 4.6.0 on macOS. It raised ten findings, `CPS-1` to `CPS-10`, concentrated at the
boundaries of the statistics rather than in their core: what counts as a replicate, what
happens on the non-default axis, and what happens to a count of zero.

This document records every finding and what was done about it. The work landed on branch
`claude/audit-2026-09` and is released as version **0.2.0**; `CHANGELOG.md` covers the
same ground from a user's point of view, including which versions produce comparable
numbers.

## Findings

### CPS-1 (P0) — Technical plate replicates inflate `n`

**Fixed.** `run_two_group_test()` pulled every row in a group, while the paired survival
path collapsed within a replicate label first, so the two paths disagreed by construction
about what a replicate was; `run_anova()` and `run_contrast()` fitted `lm()` to the raw row
frame. Three biological replicates plated four times each therefore entered a t-test as
`n = 12` on 22 df, reporting p = 9e-11 on a 0.4-log difference where the honest test on the
three biological means gives p = 0.008. `qc_summary()` flagged the very cells being
miscounted ("duplicate replicate labels") without stopping the test.

The column mapping now asks which column identifies the *biological* replicate.
`aggregate_biological_replicates()` collapses technical measurements sharing that value to
one value per biological replicate on the log10 scale — the geometric mean of the plates,
the scale every test runs on — and is applied before the pairwise tests, the ANOVA, the
`emmeans` model, the assumption diagnostics and survival pairing. `n_a` / `n_b` report the
units compared, and every result row carries `unit_of_replication`.

A file with no such column is **not** guessed at. The banner, the figure caption, the QC
table and `unit_of_replication` all read "technical replicates only", and the README states
that those numbers must not be reported as replicate-level statistics. This is deliberate:
inventing a unit of replication would be a worse error than naming the absence of one.

*Verified independently of the app's helpers:* on three biological replicates per arm with
hand-chosen log10 means (7.00, 7.20, 6.85 against the same minus 0.4), each plated four
times with offsets summing to zero, the aggregated values reproduce the hand-computed
biological means exactly, and the app's Welch test reproduces `t.test()` on those three
means exactly (df = 4, p = 0.0493149, difference = 0.4). The pre-fix pooled test on the same
24 rows gives df = 22, p = 2.46e-06.

### CPS-2 (P0) — "Raw CFU on log axis" summarised on the wrong scale

**Fixed.** The mode summarised on the raw CFU scale while the statistics and the caption
both said log10. On wells at 1e6 / 1e7 / 1e8 the bar sat at 2.352e7 — 2.35x the geometric
mean the test analysed — `mean - SD` came out at -1.41e7 and the lower whisker was silently
clamped to `.Machine$double.eps`, drawing an error bar from the floor of a log axis across
some twenty decades.

`plot_summary()` now computes the summary on log10 in both axis modes and back-transforms
for `raw_log_axis`, so the centre is the geometric mean and the interval is multiplicative
(centre ×/÷ `10^err`), strictly positive by construction with nothing to clamp.
`error_type_caption()` names the centre statistic and the scale rather than asserting an
unqualified "mean ± SD".

*Verified independently:* on those same three wells the app now returns centre = 1e7 with
the interval [1e6, 1e8], which is the hand-derived geometric mean 10^7 ×/÷ 10^(SD = 1), and
the caption reads "bars show the geometric mean, error bars the geometric mean ×/÷ 10^SD of
log10(CFU)".

### CPS-3 (P1) — Limit of detection

**Fixed.** `cfu > 0` dropped sterile wells with no alternative, which biases a log-reduction
*towards the null*: on control 1e8 x3 against treated 1e2, 1e2 and 0, dropping the zero
reports 6.0 logs where censoring at 1 CFU/mL gives 6.667. The exclusion reached nothing the
user keeps — the manifest recorded rows retained but no drop count, figure QA had ten checks
and none about non-positive rows, and the affected row's `message` column blamed "data are
essentially constant", a different reason entirely.

The sidebar now offers substitution at a user-set LOD (default 1 CFU/mL) at the LOD or at
LOD/2, against the unchanged default of exclusion. Censored wells are marked in the plotted
frame, counted in the summary, named in the figure caption and in a new "Non-positive
counts" figure-QA check, and listed well by well in `analysis_manifest.json` and in
`lod_wells.csv` inside the bundle. A biological replicate is marked censored if any of its
plates was. A non-positive LOD falls back to exclusion rather than producing `-Inf`.

### CPS-4 (P1) — `cairo_pdf` wrote no file, silently

**Fixed.** `ggsave()` reports a graphics device that failed to open as a *warning*, not an
error, so the download handler returned cleanly having written nothing and the bundle lost
`figure.pdf` in silence. `capabilities("cairo")` returns `TRUE` on exactly the machines
where this happens (macOS without XQuartz), so it is not a usable guard.

`ggsave_checked()` asserts the post-condition instead: the file must exist, be non-empty and
begin with the magic bytes of the format requested. It falls back from `cairo_pdf` to the
base `pdf` device with a visible note about font embedding, and raises when no device in the
chain can write the format, so the bundle records it as missing rather than shipping a
zero-byte file. `tests/test_bundle.R` previously asserted only that `figure.pdf` was *in*
the archive — which the defect satisfied with an unreadable file — and now checks headers
and version stamps; `tests/test_panel_size.R` exercises `ggsave_checked()` directly,
including a device chain that cannot work.

**Still unverified:** that the PDF is *correct* on a machine where cairo does load. Cairo is
unavailable on the audit and fix machine, so the fallback path is what was exercised; the
test reports which device wrote the file.

### CPS-5 (P2) — Correction scope inert for `emmeans`

**Fixed.** `run_contrast()` hardcoded `adjust_stats(out, p_adjust, "global")` and attached no
`panel` column, so the "Within each panel/group" control had no effect for that method and
`adjust_stats()`' within-panel branch could not have fired regardless. The `emmeans` `by`
variables are now attached as `panel` and the user's scope is passed through. Tukey and
Dunnett are the deliberate exception — they carry their own family-wise control, so the
app's correction is disabled for them and the table records `p_adjust_method = none` rather
than correcting twice.

### CPS-6 (P2) — Assumption checks

**Fixed, deliberately as advice rather than as a gate.** The QC tab now reports per-cell `n`,
mean and SD on `log10(CFU)`, a Shapiro-Wilk p where one is defined, and each cell's variance
as a ratio to the narrowest — computed on the aggregated units the tests use, not on the
wells. A note appears when Student or a pooled-variance `emmeans` model is selected and the
widest cell has more than 4x the variance of the narrowest. Exported as
`assumption_diagnostics.csv`.

Nothing switches a test and nothing is gated on a p-value. At n = 3 Shapiro-Wilk has almost
no power, so a passing p is not evidence of normality and using one as a gate would be worse
than the assumption it purports to check. The cells where `shapiro.test()` errors — n < 3,
n > 5000, zero spread — are described in words rather than skipped. The README says all of
this.

### CPS-7 (P2) — ANOVA versus the pairwise family

**Fixed, with one part deferred.** The ANOVA tab now states in words which relationship
holds for the selected test family: an independent family for the t-tests, a protected
post-hoc family for Tukey and Dunnett. The rank-test family gets a Kruskal-Wallis omnibus
rather than an F test, so the two tables describe the same kind of procedure. Tukey HSD and
Dunnett-versus-control post-hocs on the same model are offered for users who want the
classical route, with the app's own correction disabled and Dunnett degrading rather than
mislabelling an all-pairs family as a versus-control one.

**Deferred: Dunn's post-hoc.** The rank path pairs a Kruskal-Wallis omnibus with a family of
pairwise Wilcoxon tests under the chosen correction, which is a legitimate standalone
procedure and is now labelled as one. Dunn's test would be the protected partner of the
Kruskal-Wallis and is a reasonable future addition; it was left out because it adds a
dependency and a third multiplicity regime to explain, for a path that is already correctly
described. The ANOVA tab says the pairwise Wilcoxon family is "the nonparametric partner of
this omnibus test, not a Dunn post-hoc", and the README repeats it.

### CPS-8 (P2) — App version string

**Fixed.** One `APP_VERSION` constant, surfaced in the UI header and written into
`analysis_manifest.json`, the bundle `README.txt` and the `recreate_figure.R` header, with
the short git SHA when the app runs from a checkout. `CITATION.cff` was bumped to match.
`tests/test_bundle.R` asserts the stamps are present. A bundle that cannot name the code
that produced it is not reproducible, which is the whole claim of that export.

### CPS-9 (P3) — CSV dialects

**Fixed.** `read_csv()` ran with the default locale, so a European export (`1,5E+08` with
`;` separators) parsed to a character column, then to `NA`, and was dropped wholesale by the
`!is.na(cfu)` filter — loudly, via the drop banner, but with no way to load the file at all.
A **CSV dialect** control offers comma, semicolon/decimal-comma and tab, defaulting to
sniffing the delimiter from the first few lines; an explicit choice overrides detection. The
dialect used is recorded in the manifest, and `tests/test_column_matching.R` covers all
three plus the detection.

### CPS-10 (P3) — Arithmetic columns beside geometric ones

**Fixed.** The arithmetic columns are kept — some readers genuinely want them — but renamed
to `arithmetic_mean_cfu` / `arithmetic_sd_cfu` / `arithmetic_sem_cfu` so a column lifted out
of `summary.csv` cannot be mistaken for the quantity the figure draws and the tests analyse.
On three wells at 1e6, 1e7 and 1e8 those differ by a factor of 3.7. The geometric side now
carries its own dispersion as a multiplicative factor (`geometric_sd_factor`, with
`geometric_mean_low` / `geometric_mean_high`), where before it had none at all.

**This is a breaking change for downstream scripts** that read `mean_cfu`, `sd_cfu` or
`sem_cfu` by name. It was taken anyway because the previous names were the whole mechanism
of the error.

## Test status

All six suites pass on R 4.6.0:

```
tests/test_statistics.R        ALL STATISTICS TESTS PASSED
tests/test_end_to_end.R        ALL END-TO-END TESTS PASSED
tests/test_bundle.R            ALL BUNDLE TESTS PASSED
tests/test_panel_size.R        ALL PANEL SIZE TESTS PASSED
tests/test_column_matching.R   ALL CSV DIALECT CHECKS PASSED
tests/test_survival.R          ALL SURVIVAL TESTS PASSED  (private gp75 section skipped)
```

`test_bundle.R` failed its `contains figure.pdf` check before this work; that failure was
CPS-4 and is now fixed. `test_statistics.R` re-derives each expected value from first
principles or a hand-worked example rather than from the app's own helpers, so a bug that is
self-consistent across the app still fails it.

## Still not verified

Carried forward from the audit; none of it was resolved by this work.

- **The real `gp75` dataset.** `test_survival.R` skips its private-file section when the
  file is not present next to the repo, so the survival and pairing logic is verified only
  against synthetic and dummy data.
- **`figure.pdf` content where cairo *does* load.** See CPS-4.
- **GIF export** (`gganimate` / `gifski`) and **PowerPoint export** (`officer` / `rvg`) were
  not exercised; those packages are not installed on the audit machine.
- **Live Shiny interaction** — annotation dragging, preset round-trips through a browser and
  reactivity under rapid input changes. `test_end_to_end.R` drives the server via
  `testServer` only.
